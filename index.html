Three Simple Rules

  • 🎯
    You CAN finish it today No "become an astronaut" — something real, something doable.
  • ✅
    You'll KNOW when it's done You can check it off. "Did I do it? Yes!"
  • ❤️
    It matters to YOU Not because someone told you to — because you chose it.
